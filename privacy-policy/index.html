Privacy Policy for Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar Pro

Last updated: February 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar Pro (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is operated by Boulder Digital Media LLC. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when merchants install or use the App on their Shopify store.

Our App is designed to provide storefront functionality and analytics while minimizing data collection. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices outlined below.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information from Shopify

When a merchant installs the App, we access limited store data through the Shopify API, including:

  • Product and variant information
  • Order data necessary for attribution analytics
  • Store domain and technical configuration data

Important: We do not intentionally access, collect, or store personal customer information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

The App uses the following Shopify scopes:

  • read_products
  • write_products
  • read_orders
  • write_orders

Order data is used strictly for attribution reporting, including tracking clicks, conversions, and associated revenue values.

b) Usage and Analytics Data

To operate core features and improve performance, the App collects limited usage events such as:

  • Sticky bar views
  • Click events
  • Add-to-cart actions
  • Orders marked as paid (for attribution metrics)

This data is used solely for internal analytics and performance monitoring.

We do not collect personal customer identities through these events.

c) Automatically Collected Technical Information

Basic technical information may be logged automatically, including:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • IP address (for security and diagnostics only)
  • Timestamped usage logs

This information is used to maintain service reliability and prevent abuse.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information only to:

  • Provide the App’s functionality
  • Attribute clicks and purchases to sticky bar interactions
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal data.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited information only when necessary to operate the App, including:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers used to run our services
  • Legal authorities if required by law

We do not share Shopify customer personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain only the minimum data necessary to provide services.

If a merchant uninstalls the App:

  • The Shopify session associated with the store is deleted.
  • Access to shop data is revoked.
  • Any remaining technical data is retained only as required for security, legal compliance, or system integrity.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Merchant Responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for ensuring their own compliance with privacy laws and Shopify policies, including providing any required disclosures to their customers regarding installed apps.

8. International Data Transfers

Boulder Digital Media LLC is a United States-based company. Information may be processed on servers located outside the merchant’s country. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are applied to protect data during international transfers.

9. GDPR & International Privacy Rights

Where applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar privacy laws:

  • We act as a data processor for merchants using Shopify.
  • We process only the data necessary to provide App functionality.
  • Merchants or data subjects may request access, correction, or deletion of data by contacting us using the details below.

Because the App does not intentionally collect identifiable customer data, requests will generally relate to store-level technical information only.

10. Third-Party Platforms

The App integrates with Shopify. Shopify’s handling of data is governed by Shopify’s own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised “Last Updated” date.
